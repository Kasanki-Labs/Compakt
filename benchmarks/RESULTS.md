## Summary

### Where Compakt wins

- **silesia** — 0.4% smaller than the best alternative (7z-mx9)
- **widecsv** — 0.0% smaller than the best alternative (tar.xz)

### Where Compakt loses

- **sourcelike** — 342.5% larger than 7z-mx9
- **jsonlogs** — 133.4% larger than 7z-mx9
- **duplicates** — 19.0% larger than 7z-mx9
- **enwik8** — 2.6% larger than 7z-mx9
- **sourcetree** — 2.2% larger than 7z-mx9
- **sitepackages** — 0.7% larger than 7z-mx9
- **mixed** — 0.2% larger than 7z-mx9

### Reading these honestly

- `generated` corpora are synthetic and flatter every
  compressor. Treat their margins as an upper bound.
- `standard` corpora are single large files, where Compakt's
  structural advantages do not apply. Those are the numbers to
  quote against a sceptic.
- `compakt-ref` is the open reference encoder. The gap between
  it and `compakt` is what the proprietary routing engine buys.
